# Software Engineering career guide 2026 (Artefact, SE chapter), condensed

Source: *SE Career guide 2026.pdf* (22 slides, French, "Guide évaluation, Oct 2026"), read 2026-11-20. The grid wording is in `grid/`; the differences with the 2025 system are in `grid/_changes-2026-vs-2025.md`. The 2025 system is in `legacy-2025/career-system-2025.md`.

Titles (p.6): Junior Software Engineer (J0-J3), Senior (S1-S3), Lead (M1-M3, "manager"), then two tracks: generalist (Director SE) or expert (Principal engineer); then Partner, Managing Partner. The guide details the track up to Lead only. Inconsistency inside the guide: p.14 says a J1 hire "should have 5 points" while the ladder shows J1 = 0.

## Output versus outcome (p.5)
- *Output* = what you produced ("I built a SKAFF accelerator").
- *Outcome* = a result with a **measurable positive impact for Artefact**, on project delivery, Chapter SE or client development ("used x times on missions, cuts dev time by y weeks").
- Expected by grade: **Junior**: outcome on project delivery, output on Chapter SE, N/A for client development. **Senior**: outcome on project delivery and Chapter SE, output on client development. **Lead**: outcome on all three.

## Badges and points (p.8-9)
- Expertises: Software Developer, Site Security & Reliability Engineer (SSRE), Architect, Machine Learning Engineer, Analytics Engineer. Growth: Technical Project Lead, Pre-Sales Engineer, Engineering Manager. (The 2026 grid PDF has no grid for the three Growth expertises, see `grid/_changes-2026-vs-2025.md`.)
- Four badge levels per expertise: L1 delivers well under supervision (client reach: technical team; Artefact reach: chapter; **4 pts**); L2 self-reliant and supervisor (technical and business team; chapter; **10 pts**); L3 shares best practices (client management; tech community; **16 pts**); L4 evangelist (client direction; growth tribe and outside; **28 pts**).

## Promotion mechanics (p.7, p.11-12)
- Badges, obtained at the evaluations, give points. **Intergrade** change (J2 to J3): points. **Grade** change (J3 to S1, S to M, M to D): points **and** soft requirements. Bonus points: at most 2 pts, for someone close to the threshold with a very good semester; they are an advance on future badges.
- Soft criteria (assessed in Talent Development by self review, peer reviews and counsellor review, then the evaluation committee of M1+ members, HR and sometimes mission directors/partners): Client Development, People Management, Project Management, Communication (written and oral), Business sense, Technical Conviction. Grid: "Soft skill grid" (separate sheet, not read).

## Badge dossier (p.10)
- **One** dossier for **all** badges requested (official template); **max 2 pages per project** (diagrams, charts, illustrations excluded) and **3 relevant projects** maximum; precise and succinct.
- Review by the leads of the requested expertise: Strong "Yes" (~30%: badge granted without oral, result sent before the official return), Oral (~60%: 20 min for level 1, 30 min for level 2 and above, at least 2 leads), Strong "No" (~10%, to avoid: the counsellor should discourage a request when the level is not reached). In doubt, loop back with members of the expertise.

## Process (p.13-14)
- Evaluations every six months, **December and June**. Steps: reviewer selection (2-4 reviewers, always the N+1 and everyone worked with at length, ideally from SE and other chapters; validate with the counsellor first), self/peer reviews (2 weeks), counsellor review (2 weeks; visible one month after the evaluations), evaluation committee.
- Eligibility: more than 3 months of permanent contract. Ask for feedback right after a mission ends, not at semester end.
- Hires above J0 without assigned badges: the points at hiring are an advance on the next evaluation.

## Counsellor role (p.16-19)
30 minutes every 2 weeks; the counsellor knows the counsellee's badges, client and mood, builds an improvement plan, raises negative feedback, shares requested badges with top management before the evaluations, re-reads junior dossiers, presents the one-pager at the committee ("a messenger, not a lawyer"), and shares results only after official validation. A counsellor is at least J3 with 6 months of seniority.
