# Software Engineering Career System v2025 (Artefact, SE chapter)

Source: *SOFTWARE ENGINEER - CAREER SYSTEM v2025.pdf* (11 slides), condensed. The skill grid (`grid/`) holds the per-skill wording.

## Process
- Skills are assessed **twice a year**, via the 360 system, with reviews filled on Talent Development.
- Each talent has a **counselor** (more senior) who presents their reviews to the **evaluation committee**.
- Blockers for promotion or variable pay: being a poor team player (disagreeing is fine, fighting is not); skipping internal processes (happiness survey, mission report, timesheets).

## Ladder (points required → level)
Intern · L1 6 · L2 14 · L3 20 · L4 30 · L5 45 · L6 60 · L7 80 · L8 95 · L9 110 · L10 130 · L11 150.
Titles: Software Engineering Intern → Software Engineer → Senior Software Engineer → Lead Software Engineer → Principal Engineer (technical-expert alternative) / Engineering Director.
Role stages: *Learn & participate* (junior; variable pay on internal contribution) → *Convince & coach* (senior: delivery owner, technical point of contact for clients, mentor; variable split 50/50 internal/client impact) → *Pave the way* (lead: self-reliant, still day-to-day delivery) → *Lead the way*.

## Badges and points
A **badge** = one skill at one level of mastery. Points per level: **L1 = 4, L2 = 10, L3 = 16, L4 = 28**.

| Kind | Skills |
|---|---|
| **Expertise** | Software Developer · Architect (Cloud) · Machine Learning Engineer (shared with Data Scientists) · Analytics Engineer (shared with Data Analysts) · Site Security & Reliability Engineer (SSRE) |
| **Growth** | Technical Project Lead (grid name: *Project Tech Lead*) · Pre-Sales Engineer · Engineering Manager |

Expertise skills in one line each:
- Software Developer: code for clients (apps, data pipelines), almost never front-ends.
- SSRE: deployment, operation, security of what is shipped.
- Architect: designs data and IT architectures, guides the client.
- ML Engineer: helps data scientists ship models faster with MLOps and LLMOps.
- Analytics Engineer: data pipelines in all directions: exploration to production, documentation to data-quality monitoring.
- Technical Project Lead: leads (sometimes cross-expertise) technical teams with lean and agile practices.

## What the levels mean
| Level | Expectation | Reach (influence needed) | Reach scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivers well under supervision | Technical team | Chapter |
| 2 | Self-reliant, and supervises others | Technical and business team | Chapter |
| 3 | Shares best practices | Client management | Tech community |
| 4 | Evangelist | Client direction | Growth tribe and outside |

Each grid cell has four parts: **Definition** (definition of done), **Reach** (influence level), **Examples** (non-exhaustive facts linked to the milestone; *one doesn't need to tick all the boxes*), **Experience** (seniority in the skill).

## Badge dossier conventions (from the owner's own notes and past dossiers)
- At most **3 relevant projects**, at most **2 pages per project** (diagrams, charts, illustrations excluded). Precise and succinct: synthesis shows height on a subject.
- Dossier sections: executive summary · business context and challenges · methodology, responsibilities and outcomes · sources of pride · what did not work and what to do differently · links (repos, presentations, techtex).
- Valued content: architecture diagrams, ROI / impact / productivity metrics.
- One file per badge request. Past dossiers are written in French; a "Strong Yes" dossier included counts (commits, MRs created/reviewed) and linked code-review examples.
