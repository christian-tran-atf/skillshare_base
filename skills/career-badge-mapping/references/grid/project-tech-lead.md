# Technical Project Lead (growth)

> Source: *SE Skill Grid 2025 - Soft. Eng Tech Badges* (H1 2025 refresh), extracted from the PDF. Level 1..4 = 4/10/16/28 points. Examples are non-exhaustive: nobody ticks every box. Wrapped lines inside *Outcome Examples* cells may be merged into one bullet by the extraction.

### Expectations

**Level 1**
- I ensure delivery of software engineering streams (validate the pre- requisites, create tickets, review code, plan adoption)
- I pro-actively communicate to your reporting chain (provide regular updates on progress and potential roadblocks, manage expectations and address concerns)
- I provide technical guidance and support to junior engineers

**Level 2**
- I frame and deliver software engineering streams autonomously (gather requirements and define the scope of work, design technical architecture, write design documents and document technical decisions, estimate effort and create a roadmap, ensure code quality and adherence to best practices, manage risks and dependencies)
- I lead and mentor a small team of junior engineers (delegate tasks effectively, foster a collaborative and positive team environment, make sure the team knows on what they should work and why they work on it, identify skills gap and point to relevant training materials or knowledgeable people)

**Level 3**
- I supervise the delivery of multiple software engineering streams involving different kinds of profiles (DS, DA, DE).
- I provide technical leadership across multiple projects or teams.
- Leverage Artefact experts when needed.
- I manage and optimize resource allocation across streams.
- I foster collaboration between different teams and departments.
- I implement and enforce best practices and coding standards across streams.
- I mentor mid-level to senior engineers.
- I go back to coding to speed up delivery when necessary.

**Level 4**
- I define and drive the technical vision for large-scale projects or the entire organization.
- I anticipate future technological trends and position the organization to leverage them.
- I act as a thought leader both within the client organization and internally.
- I mentor and develop senior engineers and tech leads.
- I make high-level architectural decisions that impact multiple projects or the entire tech stack.

### Reach

**Level 1**
- Artefact consulting Manager and client tech counterparts

**Level 2**
- Artefact manager or director, and client tech lead

**Level 3**
- Artefact partner or director, Client sponsor,

**Level 4**
- Artefact partner or director, Client C-level sponsors, community of staff and principal engineers at Artefact

### Output Examples

**Level 1**
- I setup 1:1 with project engineers and the client point of contact
- I led agile ceremonies;
- I helped engineers break down tasks;
- I defined & updated a clear technical backlog

**Level 2**
- I created a document to track issues and decisions of the project;
- I created comprehensive technical design documents;
- I have precisely defined deliverables;
- I ensure high-quality, well-tested, and documented code across different fields on the project (SQL, python, TF...)

**Level 3**
- Same as L2 but on multiple streams.

**Level 4**
- I create long-term technical vision and strategy documents;
- I created architectural blueprints for data platforms;
- Presentations at tech events; Contribution to the technical base knowledge (Kode); Sponsor internal projects and act as a guide when needed

### Outcome Examples

**Level 1**
- Streams delivered on time and within budget Positive client feedback on delivered features and communication Increased team velocity and efficiency in delivering streams Junior engineers demonstrate improved coding skills and understanding of best practices due to mentorship

**Level 2**
- Growth and development of junior team members Improved team performance and increased velocity Most deadlines met at stream level

**Level 3**
- Save development time, leveraging the work from another stream Reduce time dedicated to maintenance thanks to a technological alignment and common practices Make engineers more effective thanks to knowledge sharing and cross-team collaboration

**Level 4**
- Enhance reputation of Artefact within the tech community Attraction and retention of top tech talents Successfully deliver innovative and high-impact initatives for our clients
