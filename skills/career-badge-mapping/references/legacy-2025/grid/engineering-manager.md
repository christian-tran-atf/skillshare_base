# Engineering Manager (growth)

> Source: *SE Skill Grid 2025 - Soft. Eng Tech Badges* (H1 2025 refresh), extracted from the PDF. Level 1..4 = 4/10/16/28 points. Examples are non-exhaustive: nobody ticks every box. Wrapped lines inside *Outcome Examples* cells may be merged into one bullet by the extraction.

### Expectations

**Level 1**
- I contribute to a sub mandate of the chapter.
- I am part of the interviewers pool.
- I coach one counsellee.

**Level 2**
- I lead a sub-mandate of the chapter
- I train other data engineers to become interviewers
- I coach several councellees.

**Level 3**
- I lead a key mandate of the chapter
- I supervise several sub-mandates

**Level 4**
- I own financial targets of the chapter
- I lead staffing & optimize it
- I supervise mandates

### Reach

**Level 1**
- sub mandate owner, artefact data engineers

**Level 2**
- Key mandate owner, chapter leads

**Level 3**
- HR, CTO

**Level 4**
- Head of HR, CTO, top management

### Output Examples

**Level 1**
- I track progress of coached engineers (Respect regular 1:1 with them)
- I deliver assets under supervision in a mandate
- I followed interviews trainings & created my test cases Raise alerts when needed regarding councellee

**Level 2**
- I raised meaningful alerts when needed for councellees.
- I provided a critical eye on their experiences to project them on the skill grid accurately
- I defined goals/roadmap of your mandate with chapter leads
- I reviewed interview exercises from new recruiters

**Level 3**
- I defined goals/roadmap of your mandate with chapter leads
- I structured career tracks on your expertise
- I created a roadmap with goals for sub mandates.

**Level 4**
- I monitored staffing rate & GM of the chapter
- I raised alerts when needed find solution with the team and make sure they are applied in the chapter.
- I provided goals for each mandate
- I had regular touchpoints with HR

### Outcome Examples

**Level 1**
- Training delivered to some data engineers with good feedbacks.
- Assets created under mandate used by the chapter.
- Deliver transparant & concise manager review. 2+ interviews conducted with insightful scorecard.

**Level 2**
- Reach goals aligned with the business on the mandate (increase number of campus meetings, get partnership for a cloud provider...) 2+ trained interviewers

**Level 3**
- Reach goals aligned with the business on the mandate (decrease hiring lead time, trained 10+ engineers & get good feedbacks...)

**Level 4**
- Reach KPIs provided by top management (margin, growth, pyramid balance of the team, churn...)
- Key mandates achieved their goals
