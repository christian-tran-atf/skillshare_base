# Pre-sales Engineer (growth)

> Source: *SE Skill Grid 2025 - Soft. Eng Tech Badges* (H1 2025 refresh), extracted from the PDF. Level 1..4 = 4/10/16/28 points. Examples are non-exhaustive: nobody ticks every box. Wrapped lines inside *Outcome Examples* cells may be merged into one bullet by the extraction.

### Expectations

**Level 1**
- I produce content (slides, architecture, demo...) for a pitch based on guidelines from a supervisor in autonomy.
- I translate clearly implications between technical choices & business implications on my scope to non-tech people.

**Level 2**
- I lead technical pre-sales discussions for small and well-scoped deals.
- I develop and present standard solution architectures to clients.
- I work closely with account managers to refine proposals.

**Level 3**
- I mentor junior sales engineers on best practices.
- I identify patterns in customer needs and refines reusable sales assets.
- I lead technical workshops and deep-dive sessions with clients.
- I identify opportunities during missions.

**Level 4**
- I define and own an offer sales strategy from a technical perspective
- I proactively identifies new business opportunities and market trends.
- I lead sales opportunities without account manager supervision.

### Reach

**Level 1**
- Tech lead supervising the RFP & Consultants.

**Level 2**
- Artefact partners & directors, Client tech profiles

**Level 3**
- Artefact partners & directors, Client tech profiles

**Level 4**
- Artefact partners & managing partner, CDO, CTO.

### Output Examples

**Level 1**
- I conducted a basic PoC setups showcasing tools proposed in the RFP (e.g., GenAI PoC, DP on Databricks...).
- I drafted technical sections for RFP responses.

**Level 2**
- I produce technical solution slides and architecture diagrams.
- I create well structured slides with a clear message & story telling.
- I made initial cost estimations and sizing recommendations for data workloads.
- I proposed team set-up & sizing for the project.

**Level 3**
- I built strong relationships with key decision-makers in client organizations.
- I engaged with senior technical stakeholders (CTOs, Heads of Data).
- I standardized proposal templates and reusable technical content.
- I created a new offer

**Level 4**
- I represented the company at industry events and conferences.
- I owned and drove multiple technical proposals even simultaneously.
- I closed high-value enterprise deals independently.

### Outcome Examples

**Level 1**
- Successful PoCs that demonstrate business value in time.
- Accurate and structured understanding of the topic provided to non- technical teams.

**Level 2**
- Successfully close small and mid-sized deals with minimal rework & supervision.
- Minimal drift on missions sized.

**Level 3**
- Win a total of 1m+ in one year Win 3+ RFP in one year Find new opportunities with account lead

**Level 4**
- Win a total of 2m+ in one year Win 5+ RFP in one year.
- Bring new opportunities without partner intervention.
